### This Mac
- Control: `bash devices/mac/mac.sh <cmd>`
- Commands: `volume <0-100>`, `mute`, `unmute`, `say "<text>"` (speaks from the laptop),
  `open "<App>"`, `url <https://...>`, `music play|pause|next|previous|status` (Apple Music),
  `spotify play|pause|next|previous|status|uri <spotify:track:...>`, `lock`, `sleep-display`, `battery`,
  `notify "<text>"` (on-screen notification).
