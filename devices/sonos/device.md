### Sonos speaker ("Living Room")
- Control: `node devices/sonos/sonos.js <cmd>`
- Commands: `status`, `play`, `pause`, `next`, `previous`, `volume <0-100>`, `volup [n]`, `voldown [n]`
  (default 10), `mute`, `unmute`, `uri <stream url>`.
- To start specific music on Sonos, Alexa usually can (if Sonos is linked in Alexa): it only reached
  you because Alexa failed, so prefer `play` / Spotify Connect from the Mac, and tell the user if you can't.
